FORM DATA / SEPTEMBER 2026

Privacy & form data

We collect only what the three participation forms request, use it for the stated purpose, and keep individual answers private.

This notice applies to the Youth Consultation, Share a Project or Case, and Contribute to AI×SD forms. Participation is voluntary and limited to people aged 18 or older. We ask you to confirm eligibility; we do not collect your date of birth.

What each form collects and why

Consultation: location or “prefer not to say,” role, interests, experience, barriers, requested resources, priorities, and optional written comments. We collect a follow-up email only if you agree to relevant contact. A separate public acknowledgement choice may collect a display name without a follow-up email. We may analyse answers in aggregate to inform a public consultation brief. We will not publicly attribute an individual answer or quote free text without separate, explicit permission.

Project submission: project details, stage, topics, needs, links, publication preference, and the submitter’s name and email. We use these to review and map work and, only according to the selected permission, consider a public brief, collection, or directory. Submission does not imply endorsement or partnership.

Contributor pathway: contact details, field, interests, availability, optional profile links, and a required small first contribution. An authorized person reviews each submission and records whether Contributor status is approved or declined. We normally approve unless a submission violates basic standards of respectful and lawful participation. We may contact you about specific work. Submission does not create community membership or a representative role.

Public acknowledgement

Each form separately asks whether you want your contribution acknowledged on the Contributors page. You can choose a public name, a public name and affiliation, or no listing. You may also provide a region for display. This choice is separate from consultation follow-up, permission to publish a project or case, and permission to quote an answer. Choosing a listing never publishes your email, form answers, project details, or profile links.

You may submit before any review. A person checks the received contribution and your listing choice once before confirming it. If you opted in, that confirmation permits the acknowledgement to appear. Approved listings show only the fields you explicitly selected and the type and month of contribution. A listing disappears when its source response is deleted at the 12-month limit, or earlier if you ask us to remove it. You may change your listing choice by contacting us below.

Separation and access

Responses are stored in three separate tables. We record the broad route by which a person reached a form, such as “website” or “consultation,” without linking identities across forms. We do not add a cross-form person ID, device fingerprint, or IP address to the research records. Form responses and contact addresses are not publicly searchable. Access is limited to people operating this initiative who need to review the relevant form.

Storage and retention

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form responses are sent over HTTPS to a Supabase project hosted in Sydney, Australia. We retain submitted responses for 12 months. A daily deletion task removes records once they are older than 12 months, so deletion may occur up to one day after that point. Pseudonymous rate-limit records become eligible for deletion after 48 hours and are cleared by the same daily task. Hosting providers may keep operational logs under their own policies; those logs are separate from our research tables.

Withdrawal, correction, and deletion

Email youthaisd.stunned539@slmails.com to ask about your data, correct it, withdraw from follow-up, or request deletion. Include the submission reference shown on the thank-you page, if you have it. Save that reference yourself; it is not a cross-form identity. If you did not provide contact details and lose the reference, we may not be able to identify your particular response reliably. We will verify requests before acting on records that contain another person’s contact information.

Public reporting and links

Consultation findings may be reported as aggregate counts and themes, with subgroup sizes shown where useful. Project information is considered for public use only according to the permission chosen on that form; “contact me first” means we will ask before publishing, and “internal use only” means we will not publish it. We do not upload files through these forms. We do not send answer content to analytics. The success pages offer optional links to other forms; using them does not join your answers together.

Contact

The Youth AI for Sustainable Development Initiative is an independent, early-stage initiative. For questions about these forms or data handling, contact youthaisd.stunned539@slmails.com.
