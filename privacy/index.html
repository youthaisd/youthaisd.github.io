Privacy / V0.2

How information will be handled.

YouthAISD is an independent youth-led initiative in formation. We aim to collect only what is needed for each participation route.

Registration and form submissions remain closed while the contact channel, storage region, retention period and deletion process are finalized. This notice will be updated before personal information is collected.

What a JOIN form would collect

Name, email address, country or region, institution or independent status, current role, areas of interest, and any optional profile link or note you choose to provide. The directory opt-in and acknowledgment of membership terms are recorded separately.

Why we would use it

To maintain a private community membership record and, where relevant, communicate about YouthAISD consultations, discussions, projects and future activities. JOIN does not create a user account, password, organizational office or representative role.

Public directory choices

Email addresses will not be displayed publicly. A future directory entry would require your explicit opt-in and manual approval before publication. Declining the directory has no effect on membership. No membership record is automatically published.

Other participation forms

LISTEN responses may be analysed in aggregate for a consultation brief. Individual answers are not publicly attributed without separate permission. MAP submissions may inform future research or case collections according to the permission selected in that form. CONTRIBUTE submissions are used to consider specific opportunities for substantive work. These records are kept separately; YouthAISD does not create a hidden identity link across forms.

Storage, retention and requests

Responses are intended to be stored in private tables in a Supabase project, with public browser access to those tables disabled. The actual storage region, retention period and a private project contact address for correction, withdrawal or deletion requests will be published here before submissions open. Until then, please do not send personal information through the forms.

Independence

Joining YouthAISD does not imply endorsement by a university, employer or other institution. YouthAISD has no affiliation with or endorsement by UNEP, IETC, CYMG, BCRC China or the United Nations.
