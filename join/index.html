What membership means

Membership is open.
Recognition follows contribution.

Membership is a lightweight way to stay connected with YouthAISD and its emerging community. Members may be invited to consultations, discussions, collaborative projects and future activities.

Membership does not confer organizational office, representative authority, contributor status, institutional affiliation or endorsement. Substantive roles emerge through actual work and responsibility.

Joining is different from contributing: JOIN is community affiliation; CONTRIBUTE expresses interest in specific project work.

A few details

Membership is intended for students, early-career researchers, practitioners and other young people interested in AI and sustainable development. An independent affiliation is welcome.
