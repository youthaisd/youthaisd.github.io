## Brief

**Beyond AI Literacy: Youth Participation in Hong Kong's AI and Sustainable Development Agenda**  
*A Rapid Review of Hong Kong Government Policy Documents, 2025–2026*

The brief reviews seven current or foundational policy documents plus official UN target descriptions and links its claims to 17 verified policy evidence records.

## Three main observations

1. **AI literacy and youth capacity:** Hong Kong has a specified school AI-literacy framework, teacher CPD requirements, public AI training commitments, and a future target of more than 200 AI courses and activities by Q1 2028.
2. **Youth participation in AI innovation:** Government documents name AI experience activities, research internships, a young-researcher category, and expanded Talent Foundry training opportunities. Some pathways are broad research or career measures rather than AI-specific.
3. **Responsible AI and sustainable development:** Government documents describe AI risk-governance measures and a procurement declaration pilot. The reviewed texts do not specify a standing youth role in AI governance or evaluation; this is a bounded finding, not proof that no channel exists elsewhere. Environmental energy targets are treated as a separate policy strand.
