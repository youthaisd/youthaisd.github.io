Policy Brief 001

Sources & Methodology

Verification record for the rapid review

Review scope

English-first review of selected official Hong Kong policy documents released in 2025–2026, with the 2022 Youth Development Blueprint used as background. Research and access cut-off: 9 October 2026. This is a documentary review, not an implementation evaluation.

Claims are linked to evidence IDs in the brief and recorded in the CSV matrix with source title, date, issuing body, section or page, short quotation, measure, thematic relevance, analytical SDG mapping, implementation status, official URL, and verification status.

Policy statements are distinguished from YouthAISD interpretation and proposals. Targets, funding, and pilots are not treated as completed outcomes. SDG mappings are YouthAISD analytical correspondences, not government classifications.
