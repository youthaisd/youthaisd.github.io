# Sources and access record

**Research cut-off and access date:** 9 October 2026.  
All policy materials below are official Hong Kong SAR Government or United Nations sources. Direct English-language originals were prioritised. The brief's SDG mappings are YouthAISD analytical mappings, not government classifications.
